1. Introduction

GoScape ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how GoScape handles information when you use the GoScape mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Location Information

GoScape may request access to your device's location to provide location-based travel features, including:

  • Finding tourist attractions and places near you.
  • Providing location-based recommendations.
  • Helping you plan trips and routes.

Location information used for these features is processed on your device and is not stored by GoScape as a personal location history.

You can disable location permission through your device settings. Some location-based features may not function correctly without location access.

Personal Information

GoScape does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address to use the application.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

GoScape does not intentionally collect or store:

  • Names
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Precise location history
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos from your device
  • Financial or payment information

GoScape does not currently use advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Third-Party Services

GoScape may communicate with third-party services and APIs to provide travel information, maps, images, and other destination-related content.

These services may receive information necessary to fulfill the requested functionality. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

GoScape does not sell your personal information.

5. Data Storage

GoScape does not maintain a personal user account database.

Location information used for location-based functionality is processed on the user's device and is not retained by GoScape as a personal location history.

Saved trip information, where applicable, may be stored locally on your device.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

GoScape is a general travel and discovery application. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to GoScape, please contact us using the email address below.

8. Your Choices

You can control location access through your device's Android settings.

You may also stop using the application or uninstall it at any time.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when GoScape's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated version will include a revised effective date.
